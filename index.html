About

My research is on Physical AI: getting robots to see, decide and apply the right force in real environments. My Ph.D. work applies this to robotic curtain wall installation on high-rise buildings, covering lightweight segmentation, markerless 6D pose estimation and safe, compliant control learned with reinforcement learning.

I come from mechanical, aerospace and control engineering, and I'm increasingly interested in vision-language-action models and imitation learning for generalist manipulation. I also teach engineering at Monash and co-supervise student research projects.

Research interests

  • Generalisable, data-efficient robot learning for contact-rich manipulation across diverse tasks and object geometries
  • Safe, compliant control for physical human-robot interaction and collaboration in shared workspaces
  • End-to-end perception-to-action learning, including vision-language-action models
  • Learning-based and adaptive control, including reinforcement learning for manipulation
  • Computer vision, semantic segmentation, pose estimation and sim-to-real transfer

Publications

Lightweight segmentation model for automated facade installation in high-rise buildings

Hassen, Arashpour & Abdi · Advanced Engineering Informatics, 2025

LANet, a lightweight attention network that segments curtain wall module frames so a robot can find installation locations. It reaches 84.26% mIoU with 1.92M parameters at 262 FPS on GPU and 19 FPS on CPU, matching heavier baselines at about one-seventh the parameters.

Lightweight vision-based 6D pose estimation of curtain wall module installation slot

Hassen, Arashpour & Abdi · Expert Systems with Applications, 2026

A markerless, monocular pipeline for locating where a curtain wall module goes. LANet segments the scene, corners are extracted with a rule-based algorithm, and a PnP solver estimates the pose of a reference panel that is propagated to the target slot. A learned SE(3) residual correction cuts PnP translation error about 6.7× and rotation error about 14.3×.

  • 6D pose estimation
  • PnP
  • Construction robotics
